Reconnect PubSub in the mining loop and skip non-earning campaigns

Two fixes prompted by a real incident where a miner sat for 3.5 hours on
the only campaign eligible for its account, earning nothing:

- PubSub was connected only at miner start and on auth refresh, never
from the mining loop, despite a comment claiming otherwise. If the
socket dropped (or its initial connect failed) the miner ran blind on
real-time progress for the rest of the session, relying on polling
alone. startWatching failures now attempt one reconnect + retry.

- When a campaign genuinely stalls (no verified progress, no external
claim) and there's no failover streamer to try, the miner kept
re-selecting that same campaign's streams forever because it was the
only eligible one. After nonEarningStallThreshold consecutive such
stalls it now puts the campaign on a cooldown so candidateCampaigns
skips it, letting the miner find other work or report idle. Any
verified progress clears the streak and cooldown.

Adds tests for registerGenuineStall and isOnStallCooldown. Ships as
1.34.1 (1.34 was already released without these): new 1.34.1 release
notes, MARKETING_VERSION bumped to 1.34.1, build 2026072309. The
released 1.34 notes and the appcast are left untouched.
