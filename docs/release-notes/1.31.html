SwiftMiner 1.31

Draft release notes for work in progress

A performance-focused update that noticeably lowers CPU usage, adds game-specific failover streamers, and improves how SwiftMiner handles tricky campaign states.

⚡ Lower CPU Usage

Less repeated work while mining, refreshing campaigns, and displaying the Overview.

  • Stops each miner activity card from repeatedly rebuilding the same status snapshot during a single redraw, substantially reducing Overview work when several accounts are visible.
  • Caches decoded game preferences instead of repeatedly parsing and normalising the saved preference data during SwiftUI updates.
  • Builds prioritised campaign data and exclusion lookups once per redraw instead of recalculating them several times.
  • Reuses Twitch date parsers across campaign refreshes, reducing short CPU spikes and temporary allocations while processing campaign and reward dates.
  • Pauses animated campaign-card backgrounds whenever SwiftMiner is not the active app, avoiding unnecessary background rendering.

🔄 Failover Streamers

Give important games a trusted fallback when progress stalls.

  • Adds an optional failover streamer to each configured game rule.
  • When progress genuinely stalls, SwiftMiner can switch to that game's failover streamer after confirming the channel is live and advertising the correct campaign.
  • Adds cooldown handling so a failover streamer that also stalls is not retried repeatedly.

🎮 Drops & Campaigns

Clearer handling for prioritised, unlinked, and subscription-required campaigns.

  • Keeps prioritised games visible in Drops Active even when Twitch reports the active campaign as unlinked.
  • Preserves subscription-required rewards when same-game campaigns are grouped, including the credit-card badge and Sub label.
  • Shows Needs Sub and Paid Twitch sub required instead of presenting subscription rewards as generic locked rewards.
  • Stops a same-game watch campaign appearing under Up Next when the currently visible live campaign requires a subscription.
  • Hides unrelated public campaigns that are neither linked nor prioritised, keeping Drops Active focused on campaigns that matter to your accounts.

⚙️ Settings & Polish

  • Moves artwork, tips, Activity Log icons, and visual-effect controls into a dedicated Appearance settings tab.
  • Adds a single Use simpler visual effects option for quickly disabling row animations and animated or multi-colour status icons.
  • Keeps Integrations available in Settings even when Discord support is disabled, and moves the Discord enable switch into that tab.
  • Adds Essential, Balanced, Everything, and Custom presets for Discord DM notifications.
  • Simplifies account management around the current Add Account flow.
  • Adds an in-app help link and detailed guide for game priorities, exclusions, account-specific ordering, and failover streamers.
  • Adds a clickable swiftminer.app link to About SwiftMiner, plus Website and Help menu items for quick access to the site and hosted guide.
