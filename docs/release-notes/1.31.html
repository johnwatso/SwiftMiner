SwiftMiner 1.31

Version 1.31 · Build 2026062409

A substantial refinement focused on lower resource use, resilient mining, clearer campaign states, and easier configuration.

⚡ Performance

SwiftMiner does less repeated work while sitting quietly in the background.

  • Noticeably lowers CPU usage by eliminating repeated miner-card and campaign-feed calculations.
  • Caches decoded game preferences and reuses Twitch date parsers.
  • Pauses campaign-card animation while SwiftMiner is inactive.

📡 Resilient Stream Selection

Miners recover more gracefully when a preferred stream stops making progress.

  • Adds optional game-specific failover streamers that are verified before use.
  • Activates failover only after genuine progress stalls and applies cooldowns to avoid repeatedly retrying a bad fallback.

🎟️ Clearer Drops States

Subscription-gated and unlinked campaigns are represented more accurately.

  • Keeps prioritised games visible in Drops Active even when Twitch reports the active campaign as unlinked.
  • Preserves paid-sub reward styling with the credit-card badge, Sub thumbnail label, and pink Needs Sub status.
  • Updates campaign details to say Needs Sub and Paid Twitch sub required instead of showing a generic locked state.
  • Stops Overview from presenting a same-game watch campaign under Up Next while the visible live campaign is subscription-gated.
  • Filters unlinked, non-prioritised public campaigns out of Drops Active.

⚙️ Simpler Settings

Configuration is grouped around the decisions people actually make.

  • Adds a dedicated Appearance tab and a combined simpler-visual-effects option.
  • Keeps Integrations visible and replaces individual Discord DM switches with notification presets plus a Custom mode.
  • Simplifies account management around the current Add Account flow.

📖 Help and Navigation

Website and help resources are easier to reach from inside the app.

  • Adds a clickable swiftminer.app link to About SwiftMiner and a SwiftMiner Website menu item.
  • Adds SwiftMiner Help to the Help menu.
  • Adds an in-app help link and detailed guide for priorities, exclusions, account-specific ordering, and failover streamers.
