SwiftMiner 1.31.1

Version 1.31.1 · Build 2026062412

A focused update that makes the Activity Log a durable history of mining, support, audit, and app-update activity.

📚 Persistent Activity Log

Your operational history now survives app restarts.

  • Keeps the newest 1,000 Activity Log entries across app restarts instead of retaining only web audit events.
  • Preserves update checks, downloads, automatic installation plans, errors, mining events, Discord activity, and web audit entries in one timeline.
  • Makes Clear Log remove both the visible timeline and its saved history.

✨ Update History

Sparkle updates leave a clear, useful record.

  • Records a successful version transition after Sparkle installs an update and relaunches SwiftMiner.
  • Shows a dedicated macOS notification after a successful update, independently of drop notifications and quiet hours, with a shortcut to What's New.
  • Creates a version-and-build baseline the first time persistent update auditing runs.

🧪 Safer Testing

Automated tests stay isolated from your real SwiftMiner setup.

  • Isolates automated tests from saved Debug accounts, background services, notifications, updates, and miner auto-start.
