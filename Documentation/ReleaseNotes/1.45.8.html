SwiftMiner 1.45.8

Version 1.45.8 · Build 2026111013 · Prepared 10 November 2026

SwiftMiner 1.45.8 keeps recurring rewards attributed correctly across restarts, makes clearing cached history reliable during refreshes, and makes idle-miner diagnostics easier to read.

🐝 Reliable reward history

  • Repeated rewards now save their latest award date immediately, so rewards reused by daily or recurring campaigns stay attributed to the correct campaign after restarting.
  • Clearing cached drop history now discards responses from refreshes that were already running, preventing old history from reappearing when those requests finish. A later refresh can still load fresh history normally.
  • Switching accounts also discards inventory responses from the previous account, keeping each account's rewards separate.

🔎 Clearer diagnostics

  • Diagnostic exports now show how long until each miner's next campaign check, or how overdue it is, instead of showing future checks as zero seconds old.
