SwiftMiner 1.40.3

Version 1.40.3 · Build 2026090216

This hotfix makes Discord profile pictures dependable immediately after SwiftBot starts.

🐝 Discord Profile Picture Fixes

  • Linked accounts now receive their real Discord profile picture without waiting for that member to trigger a new Discord event.
  • Discord's generated placeholder is no longer treated as a selected profile picture; SwiftMiner uses the account's Twitch picture until the real Discord avatar is available.
