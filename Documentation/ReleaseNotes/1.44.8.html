SwiftMiner 1.44.8

Version 1.44.8 · Build 2026101521 · Released 15 October 2026

SwiftMiner 1.44.8 stops an account from repeatedly claiming drops that Twitch accepts but never marks as claimed. Engine 4.40.

🐝 Repeated drop claims

  • An account that wasn't linked to a game could claim the same drops over and over: one account re-claimed seven Battlefield 6 drops about every five minutes for roughly 39 hours. Twitch accepted each claim, but the drops never showed as claimed. A drop is now retried at most twice per run, then skipped.
  • SwiftMiner logs one warning naming the drop when it stops retrying, and mentions when the account doesn't appear to be linked to the game.
  • Claim counts, notifications and activity events no longer inflate, and mining cycles on the affected account no longer spend several seconds on claims every pass.
