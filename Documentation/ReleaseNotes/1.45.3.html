SwiftMiner 1.45.3

Version 1.45.3 · Build 2026102511 · Prepared 25 October 2026

SwiftMiner 1.45.3 stops reporting idle miners as unhealthy and makes the diagnostic report name the real reason followed-streamer prioritisation is off. Engine 4.45.

🐝 Accurate miner health

  • A miner with no drops left to earn is no longer marked unhealthy while it waits for its next campaign check. It now stays healthy until that check is due, and open health incidents for it are resolved as expected. A miner that misses its scheduled check is still flagged.
  • When followed-streamer prioritisation is off because an account was signed in before the setting was turned on, the diagnostic report now says the account was never granted the follow permission, instead of saying Twitch rejected the account's token. Signing the account in again grants the permission.
