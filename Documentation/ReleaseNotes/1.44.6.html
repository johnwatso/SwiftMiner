SwiftMiner 1.44.6

Version 1.44.6 · Build 2026101216 · Prepared 12 October 2026

SwiftMiner 1.44.6 switches from Twitch's deprecated Spade watch tracking to HLS playlist segment requests, restoring drop progress for all campaigns. The activity card now shows the drop Twitch is actually crediting when a stream grants multiple campaigns. Engine 4.39.

📡 HLS watch sessions replace Spade heartbeats

  • Twitch deprecated SpadeEvent watch tracking around 8 October 2026. The old minute-watched beacon still returned HTTP 204 but silently stopped crediting drop progress. SwiftMiner now fetches HLS playlist segments via HEAD requests every 20 seconds, which Twitch continues to credit. Thanks to TwitchDropsMiner and its community for identifying the change and the fix.
  • Signed playback tokens are cached for three minutes and automatically refreshed on expiry or playlist failure. Tokens and signed URLs are never logged.
  • Legacy Spade and GQL watch transports are retained as labeled fallbacks when HLS is transiently unavailable.

🎮 Concurrent campaign progress

  • When a stream grants drops for more than one campaign for the same game, the activity card now shows the drop Twitch is actually crediting rather than always displaying the scheduler's primary campaign at zero progress.
  • The subtitle names the credited campaign when it differs from the scheduled one, so parallel rewards are visible at a glance.

🐝 More time for campaigns that earn

  • After three confirmed stalls, a non-earning campaign is skipped for 30 minutes. If it keeps failing after retries, the delay increases to 60, then at most 120 minutes, reducing repeated interruptions to other work.
  • Campaigns become available for retry when their delay expires. Recovery history remains scoped to each account and campaign.

🎯 Recognise recovery from Twitch inventory

  • New progress confirmed by Twitch inventory now clears campaign stall history and restores the normal retry delay, matching recovery from real-time progress.
  • Recovered campaigns return to the eligible campaign list immediately. Repeated readings with no new progress leave recovery history intact.

🌐 Internet access for every dashboard

  • Web dashboard Internet Access and Twitch sign-in settings are available even with one miner and no SwiftBot integration. Configure your own tunnel and public address to reach your miner remotely.
  • Setup instructions explain how to use an existing tunnel without SwiftBot. SwiftBot can still register the dashboard on its tunnel automatically when paired.
