SwiftMiner 1.44.6

Version 1.44.6 · Build 2026101023 · Prepared 10 October 2026

SwiftMiner 1.44.6 gives earning campaigns more time to run when other campaigns repeatedly stall, and restores normal retries when Twitch inventory confirms new progress. Web dashboard internet setup is now available for a single miner without SwiftBot. Engine 4.38.

🐝 More time for campaigns that earn

  • After three confirmed stalls, a non-earning campaign is skipped for 30 minutes. If it keeps failing after retries, the delay increases to 60, then at most 120 minutes, reducing repeated interruptions to other work.
  • Campaigns become available for retry when their delay expires. Recovery history remains scoped to each account and campaign.

🎯 Recognise recovery from Twitch inventory

  • New progress confirmed by Twitch inventory now clears campaign stall history and restores the normal retry delay, matching recovery from real-time progress.
  • Recovered campaigns return to the eligible campaign list immediately. Repeated readings with no new progress leave recovery history intact.

🌐 Internet access for every dashboard

  • Web dashboard Internet Access and Twitch sign-in settings are available even with one miner and no SwiftBot integration. Configure your own tunnel and public address to reach your miner remotely.
  • Setup instructions explain how to use an existing tunnel without SwiftBot. SwiftBot can still register the dashboard on its tunnel automatically when paired.
