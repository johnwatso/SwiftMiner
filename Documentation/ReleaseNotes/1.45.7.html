SwiftMiner 1.45.7

Version 1.45.7 · Build 2026110713 · Prepared 7 November 2026

SwiftMiner 1.45.7 keeps Operator renewal from stalling silently, makes a miner that is waiting for a stream look like it, and shows campaign artwork straight from the cache.

🐝 Fixes

  • Operator renewal no longer stops for good when the hidden Twitch security check stalls. The check now gives up after two and a half minutes, logs the timeout and tries again, instead of quietly skipping every later renewal while the app said it was renewing.
  • A miner waiting for a live stream no longer shows a green watch-progress bar under Current status, which made it look as if it were mining. It now says it is waiting for an eligible live stream; progress is still shown in the campaign queue.
  • Campaign artwork older than an hour now appears immediately from the cache and refreshes in the background, rather than leaving blank tiles while every image downloads again.
  • The Prioritised filter on Drops now includes games matched by a wildcard priority such as Call of Duty, the same way mining already did.

✨ Interface

  • Waiting for a stream has a new icon: a TV with a magnifying glass that drifts in a small circle while the miner looks for a channel.
  • The summary cards at the top of Drops have been removed, so the page opens straight onto search, filters and campaigns.
