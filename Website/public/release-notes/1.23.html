SwiftMiner 1.23

Version 1.23 · Build 2026052419

A maintenance release for quieter operator notifications, safer settings backup, and Discord self-service controls.

🌙 Quiet Hours

Operator-facing notifications are less noisy overnight.

  • Adds quiet hours for local claim notifications and Discord DM events.
  • Applies the quiet-hours window consistently to local and Discord operator notification paths.

🧰 Settings Backup

App preferences and integration settings are easier to preserve.

  • Adds settings backup and restore for preferences, game rules, filters, quiet hours, and integration endpoints.
  • Keeps operator configuration portable without touching account authentication data.

💬 Discord Controls

Linked users can handle common miner actions without opening the app.

  • Adds Discord self-service miner controls for linked users: status, pause, resume, and refresh.
  • Updates the stable Sparkle appcast and release-note metadata for SwiftMiner 1.23 build 2026052419.
